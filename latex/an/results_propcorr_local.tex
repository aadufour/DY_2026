\subsection{Expected limits with propagator corrections}
\label{sec:results_propcorr}

% src: Giacomo (5 Nov): 9.3 uses the propcorr samples.
%      Setup: config_v9.py vs config_propcorr_v1.py (only the EFT signal differs,
%      checked with diff); no MC stat on the EFT signal (SMP-V slides 06/10, p. 12);
%      stat+syst scans, triple-differential distribution (makeSummaryPropcorr*.py,

The expected limits of Section~9.3
include propagator corrections. To show their effect, the expected limits are
compared with those obtained from EFT templates without propagator corrections
(Section~\ref{sec:eft_propcorr}). The comparison uses a simplified setup with
respect to Section~9.3: privately produced samples with lower
statistics, and no MC statistical uncertainty on the EFT templates. The two fits
differ only in the EFT templates, so the comparison isolates the effect of the
propagator corrections. Both use the triple-differential distribution and the
2018 dataset.

% src: summary plot (four-fermion rows overlap); ratio plot (values).
Figure~\ref{fig:propcorr_summary} compares the expected 68\% and 95\% CL intervals
for all operators. As expected, the intervals on the four-fermion operators,
which do not enter $\delta\Gamma_Z$, are unchanged.
Figure~\ref{fig:propcorr_widthratio} shows the ratio of the widths of the 68\% CL
intervals for the 15 operators entering $\delta\Gamma_Z$. The largest effects are
on $\mathcal{O}_{HQ}^{(1)}$ and $\mathcal{O}_{HQ}^{(3)}$ (ratio 0.22),
$\mathcal{O}_{\ell\ell}^{(1)}$ (0.25) and $\mathcal{O}_{H\ell}^{(3)}$ (0.50),
whose intervals become tighter by factors between two and five.

The interval on $\mathcal{O}_{Hd}$ becomes wider (1.57), and the one on
$\mathcal{O}_{HWB}$ slightly wider (1.16); the remaining operators change by
less than 15\%.
